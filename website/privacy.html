Tethr Link

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Tethr Link links your phone to your Windows PC. It's built so your personal data stays on your own devices. This page explains what that means in practice.

Your phone's data stays on your network

Notifications, texts, calls, contacts, photos, files, your clipboard and your screen go straight from your phone to your PC over your own Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, encrypted with TLS. They never pass through a Tethr Link server, and we can't see them. Your PC saves only what it needs to show them to you, on that PC.

No account needed

Tethr Link works fully without an account. Without one, the app sends nothing to us at all. It doesn't use analytics, ads or tracking.

The optional account

You can choose to sign in with Google, Discord or GitHub. This lets your phone add your PC without scanning a QR code, and lets your settings follow you to a new PC. If you sign in, we store:

  • From your sign-in provider: your email address and a user ID. Google, Discord or GitHub also share your name and profile picture; we don't use them.
  • For each PC you sign in on: its name, its address on your local network, its security certificate fingerprint, and a one-time pairing key that changes after it's used.
  • If settings sync is on: app preferences such as theme, notification options, muted apps, quick actions and screen sharing quality.

This is stored with Supabase, which hosts the account service. Each record can only be read by the account it belongs to. We don't sell or share it, and we only use it to provide these features.

Updates

To check for updates, the apps ask GitHub for the latest Tethr Link release. GitHub sees that request like any other web request (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Deleting your data

  • Sign out on a PC (Settings → Account) to remove that PC from your account.
  • Uninstall the apps to remove everything they saved on your devices. The PC uninstaller asks whether to delete your settings too.
  • To delete your whole account, open an issue on GitHub asking for account deletion, and we'll delete it and everything stored with it.

Children

The optional account isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Tethr Link is open source, so you can check exactly what it does in the source code.
