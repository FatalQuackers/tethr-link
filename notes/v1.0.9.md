**Fix: phones couldn't connect to the PC after updating to v1.0.8.** The PC now starts waiting for your phone before anything else, so nothing else (Bluetooth, the optional account) can hold it up.

Also:
- The PC's Logs page now says when it's ready for your phone, and why a phone couldn't connect if it tries and fails.
- Builds from the test page no longer replace themselves with the latest release.

## Install
- **Android:** download `TethrLink-android.apk` on your phone and open it.
- **iPhone:** install `TethrLink-iOS.ipa` with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly.
- **Windows (installer):** download `TethrLink-Setup-x64.exe` and run it. No admin needed.
- **Windows (portable):** download `TethrLink-windows-x64-portable.zip`, extract it anywhere and run `Pixlink.exe`.

Already have it? It updates itself.
