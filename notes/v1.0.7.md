**New: connect when you're near your PC.** Your PC sends a small Bluetooth signal, and your phone reconnects the moment it's in range instead of waiting for its next try. Most phones watch for it in the Bluetooth chip itself, so it uses almost no battery. On by default (Settings → Connect when near my PC; on the PC, Settings → Connect when my phone is nearby).

**Fixed: wrong sign-in codes.** A number from an address (like "4000 George St" in an email footer) could be copied as a code. Now a number only counts when a word like "code" or "PIN" is right next to it, and never when it looks like an address, phone number, price or order number.

**iPhone**
- Reconnects as soon as your phone's Bluetooth link to the PC comes back

## Install
- **Android:** download `TethrLink-android.apk` on your phone and open it.
- **iPhone:** install `TethrLink-iOS.ipa` with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly.
- **Windows (installer):** download `TethrLink-Setup-x64.exe` and run it. No admin needed.
- **Windows (portable):** download `TethrLink-windows-x64-portable.zip`, extract it anywhere and run `Pixlink.exe`.

Already have it? It updates itself.
