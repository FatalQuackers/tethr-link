**Fix: connect from anywhere now sets itself up from the QR code.** Before, your phone only learned how to reach your PC over the internet after connecting on the same Wi-Fi first, which school and work Wi-Fi block. Now the PC's pairing QR code includes it:

1. On the PC, open **Pair a phone** to show the QR code.
2. Scan it with your phone, even on mobile data.

Your phone pairs through the internet straight away, and connects from anywhere from then on. Every connection also refreshes these details, so they stay current.

**New: developer builds.** Testers with a developer code can get every new build as soon as it's made, updating itself: Settings → Updates → Developer builds (PC and Android).

## Install
- **Android:** download `TethrLink-android.apk` on your phone and open it.
- **iPhone:** install `TethrLink-iOS.ipa` with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly.
- **Windows (installer):** download `TethrLink-Setup-x64.exe` and run it. No admin needed.
- **Windows (portable):** download `TethrLink-windows-x64-portable.zip`, extract it anywhere and run `Pixlink.exe`.

Already have it? It updates itself.
