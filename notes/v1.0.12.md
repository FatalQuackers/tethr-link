**Steadier on school and work Wi-Fi**
- **A second internet route, through Supabase.** Some school networks block Cloudflare, where the internet relay runs. Tethr Link now also connects through Supabase, which those networks allow. It tries Cloudflare first, then Supabase, then Bluetooth. Still encrypted end to end.
- **Bluetooth backup, one tap:** your phone's home screen now shows **Set up Bluetooth backup** until it's paired. Once it is, your phone switches to Bluetooth by itself whenever the Wi-Fi drops out, and back when it returns.
- **The internet connection comes back faster** after a Wi-Fi dropout: the PC retries straight away when the network returns, and at least every minute until then.

## Install
- **Android:** download `TethrLink-android.apk` on your phone and open it.
- **iPhone:** install `TethrLink-iOS.ipa` with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly.
- **Windows (installer):** download `TethrLink-Setup-x64.exe` and run it. No admin needed.
- **Windows (portable):** download `TethrLink-windows-x64-portable.zip`, extract it anywhere and run `Pixlink.exe`.

Already have it? It updates itself.
