**New: connect from anywhere.** When your phone and PC can't reach each other directly (school or work Wi-Fi, your phone on mobile data), they now connect through the internet, like Phone Link does. It's still encrypted end to end with the same security as Wi-Fi: the relay in the middle only passes along scrambled data and can't read anything. Your phone needs to connect to the PC once (Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) to set it up. Screen sharing still needs Wi-Fi. Turn it off in Settings on the PC ("Connect from anywhere").

**Better Bluetooth**
- **Pair over Bluetooth** in one tap: on your phone, Settings → Connect using → Pair over Bluetooth. Accept the prompt on both.
- Bluetooth connections retry properly instead of giving up on the first try.

Your phone now says *why* it isn't connected (for example "Not on Wi-Fi" or "Your PC isn't online").

## Install
- **Android:** download `TethrLink-android.apk` on your phone and open it.
- **iPhone:** install `TethrLink-iOS.ipa` with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly.
- **Windows (installer):** download `TethrLink-Setup-x64.exe` and run it. No admin needed.
- **Windows (portable):** download `TethrLink-windows-x64-portable.zip`, extract it anywhere and run `Pixlink.exe`.

Already have it? It updates itself.
