**New: an optional Tethr Link account.** Everything still works without one. Sign in with Google, Discord or GitHub, and you get:
- **Add a PC without a QR code.** Sign in on your PC (Settings → Account) and on your phone, then tap "Add from my account" on the phone's pairing screen.
- **Your settings follow you.** Theme, notifications, quick actions and screen sharing settings come with you to a new PC (you can turn this off).

**New: Uninstall from inside the app.** On the PC, Settings → Uninstall (choose whether to keep your settings). On Android, Settings → Uninstall Tethr Link. On iPhone, the ⋯ menu clears everything and shows how to remove the app.

Your account only stores your PC's name, address and a one-time pairing key, locked so only you can read them. Your messages, notifications, photos and files never leave your Wi-Fi.

## Install
- **Android:** download `TethrLink-android.apk` on your phone and open it.
- **iPhone:** install `TethrLink-iOS.ipa` with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly.
- **Windows (installer):** download `TethrLink-Setup-x64.exe` and run it. No admin needed.
- **Windows (portable):** download `TethrLink-windows-x64-portable.zip`, extract it anywhere and run `Pixlink.exe`.

Already have it? It updates itself.
