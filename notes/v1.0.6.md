**Much better battery life, on your phone and your laptop.** Every feature is still here: Tethr Link just does a lot less in the background.

**New: connect when you're near your PC.** Your PC sends a small Bluetooth signal, and your phone reconnects the moment it's in range instead of waiting for its next try. Most phones watch for it in the Bluetooth chip itself, so it uses almost no battery. On by default (Settings → Connect when near my PC; on the PC, Settings → Connect when my phone is nearby).

**Fixed: wrong sign-in codes.** A number from an address (like "4000 George St" in an email footer) could be copied as a code. Now a number only counts when a word like "code" or "PIN" is right next to it, and never when it looks like an address, phone number, price or order number.

**Phone**
- Wi-Fi is no longer kept awake: Tethr Link only listens for your PC while it's actually missing
- No more retrying every 30 seconds when you're away from your PC: it waits longer (up to 10 minutes with the screen off) and reconnects straight away when you're back on Wi-Fi or turn the screen on
- Doesn't try Wi-Fi at all when you're not on Wi-Fi
- Battery level is only sent when it changes, instead of every few seconds
- Repeated and fast-updating notifications (like download progress) are sent at most every 1.5 seconds, and duplicates are skipped
- Nothing is prepared for the PC while no PC is connected
- Fixed: app icons sometimes never reached the PC

**PC**
- Efficiency mode: while Tethr Link is in the tray or minimized, Windows runs it on its power-saving cores at low priority (the green leaf in Task Manager). Notifications still arrive instantly
- Checks in with your phone less often (every 35 seconds instead of 15), so your phone's Wi-Fi can sleep
- Announces itself on the network less often once your phone is connected

**iPhone**
- Fewer check-ins while the app is open; battery only sent when it changes

## Install
- **Android:** download `TethrLink-android.apk` on your phone and open it.
- **iPhone:** install `TethrLink-iOS.ipa` with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly.
- **Windows (installer):** download `TethrLink-Setup-x64.exe` and run it. No admin needed.
- **Windows (portable):** download `TethrLink-windows-x64-portable.zip`, extract it anywhere and run `Pixlink.exe`.

Already have it? It updates itself.
